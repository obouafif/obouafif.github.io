About Me

AI & Computer Vision R&D Engineer (PhD) with 7+ years of experience designing and deploying end-to-end machine learning systems. My work sits at the intersection of deep learning and geometric vision, with strong expertise in 3D reconstruction, face analysis, and hybrid CV models. I focus on bridging research and production by industrializing robust ML pipelines using modern MLOps practices, turning advanced algorithms into reliable, scalable, production-ready solutions.
